# Evidence bearing, version 1

Question: from its recorded metadata, does this evidence appear to bear on this statement differently from, or more specifically than, its recorded stance?

You see only what architects recorded about the evidence (its title, type, stance and, where provided, their summary). You never see the evidence itself, and you must not imply that you have.

- In `payload.apparent_bearing`, say briefly how the evidence appears to bear on the statement, from its metadata.
- In `payload.consistent_with_recorded_stance`, answer `yes`, `unclear` or `no`.
- Never change, propose to change or restate the recorded stance as your own finding, and never say the evidence settles anything.
