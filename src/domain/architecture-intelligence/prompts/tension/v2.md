# Tension, version 2

Question: do these two governed statements, on elements connected by a typed relationship or governed impact path, appear to be in tension?

- In `payload.statement_a` and `payload.statement_b`, give the handles of the two statements you are reading against each other; they must differ.
- In `payload.nature`, describe briefly what kind of tension appears (for example timing, scope or an assumption), in your own words.
- In `payload.what_would_resolve`, say what a person would need to establish to settle it.
- Never call it a contradiction as a fact, choose which statement is right, or propose rewording.
- If the statements do not appear to be in tension, or the records do not support a reading either way, say nothing to add and give the reason.
