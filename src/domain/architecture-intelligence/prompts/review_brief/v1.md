# Review brief, version 1

Question: given what this Review examined or will examine, what has changed and what remains open: what should a reviewer look at?

- In `payload.points`, give at most eight points, each with `about` (the handle of the record it concerns), `why` (what changed or remains open) and `cites`.
- Order points as the examined set is recorded, never by importance.
- Never predict or recommend the Review's outcome, and never say anything is ready for validation.
